Strategy

The go-to automation platform purpose-built for small businesses - making enterprise-grade process automation accessible, affordable, and actionable without requiring technical expertise.

We give small business owners and operators the power to automate their most time-consuming processes so they can focus on growth, not grunt work - no developers or IT team required.

Visual direction

Precision Flow

A crisp, high-contrast system identity that communicates capability and reliability through disciplined geometry, clear typographic hierarchy, and a restrained color palette. Confidence through order — this platform runs like clockwork. Some small business owners — particularly those who've been burned by unreliable tools — respond to visual signals of precision and dependability over warmth. This direction uses high contrast, tight grids, and deliberate use of a single electric accent to communicate that the product is serious, dependable, and genuinely powerful without feeling out of reach.
